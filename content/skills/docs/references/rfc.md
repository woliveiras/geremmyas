# RFC

Use an RFC for a material proposal that needs review before it becomes an
accepted decision. Follow an existing repository template when present.

## Minimum structure

1. Status, owner, and review deadline.
2. Problem and evidence.
3. Goals and explicit non-goals.
4. Proposed design, interfaces, and migration.
5. Alternatives and trade-offs.
6. Security, reliability, operability, and rollback considerations.
7. Validation plan and unresolved questions.

Keep the RFC as a proposal. When the decision is accepted and is complex and
hard to reverse, record the durable decision through the ADR mode. Do not copy
the full RFC into the ADR; link it and summarize the chosen outcome.
